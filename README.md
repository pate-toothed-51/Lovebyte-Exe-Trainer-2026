## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

lovebyte.exe is a chat-based dating sim and does not use character movement or traditional exploration controls. Progress is made through conversations, DMs, group chats, streams, voice calls, minigames, and interface selections.

### Tools & Inventory

The game uses money, decorations, digital merchandise, collectibles, CGs, clips, and other unlockable content rather than a traditional combat inventory. Use the trainer's money and collection options only after the relevant game screen has loaded.

### World & Environment

The story unfolds across an in-game Discord-style interface, desktop customization screens, group chats, direct messages, VTuber streams, voice calls, and scam-detection minigames. Unlock options are best used from the corresponding menu or after saving.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no combat system in lovebyte.exe. Its main challenges involve identifying fake surveys, suspicious downloads, and scam emails, managing money, choosing dialogue responses, uncovering identities, and pursuing route-specific outcomes.
